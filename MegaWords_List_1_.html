1. Learn

Compound words are made from two smaller meaningful words. Reading the smaller parts can help you read and spell the whole word.

Quick Read

Read each word aloud. Click a word after you read it.

Notice the Parts

Say the two smaller words, then say the compound word.

2. Build Words

Choose two word parts that make a real compound word. Then type the whole word.

3. Divide & Analyze

Break each compound word into its two smaller words.

4. Spell

Before You Read

Practice these words before the timed reading:

A Busy Saturday Morning

On Saturday morning, Maya packed her backpack before breakfast. She put a notebook inside and walked outside to meet her grandfather. They planned to visit a playground near the railroad museum. On the way, they stopped for pancakes and strawberries. Maya noticed sunshine through the window and told her grandfather that the weather was perfect. At the museum, they saw an old railroad car and a display about how people traveled long ago. Afterward, they ate popcorn and walked along the sidewalk to the playground. Maya was tired when she got home, but she did not forget to put her notebook back in the classroom bag she would need on Monday.
