During a picnic on campus, a student named Phillip noticed a frantic rabbit near a basket. The rabbit had pulled a napkin from a sandwich and was hopping toward a cactus. Phillip called his friend, who thought the whole problem was fantastic. A teacher arrived and gently moved the basket away. Everyone watched the rabbit disappear into a tunnel beside the public garden. Later, the class discussed the incident and wrote about it in a notebook. Their teacher reminded them that reading longer words is easier when they divide the words into syllables and look for familiar spelling patterns.
